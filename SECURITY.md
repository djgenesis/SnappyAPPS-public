# Security Policy

**Publisher:** SnappyAI Solutions · **Report address:** snappyaisolutions@gmail.com

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please email **snappyaisolutions@gmail.com** with a description of the issue, the app name and version, steps to reproduce, and (if appropriate) a proof of concept. Do **not** open a public issue for a suspected vulnerability.

- We aim to acknowledge a report within **72 hours**.
- We will triage and, where confirmed, work on a fix and coordinate disclosure with you.
- Please give us a reasonable opportunity to fix the issue before public disclosure.
- Reports about a specific app are handled by the same address and routed to that app's private repository.

## Scope

All 20 SnappyAI Solutions Android apps and the policy documents in this repository. Out of scope: third-party services you choose to connect (your mail server, feed hosts, OAuth providers) and issues already fixed in the latest release.

## Supported releases

We support the current release and the previous minor version. Please update before reporting.

## Privacy note

The apps contain no telemetry. Diagnostic information is only ever sent by you, manually, through the user-initiated "Send diagnostics" email flow.
