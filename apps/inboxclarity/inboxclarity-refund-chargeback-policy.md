# InboxClarity — Refund and Chargeback Policy

**Effective:** 21 November 2026 · **Publisher:** SnappyAI Solutions · **Contact:** snappyaisolutions@gmail.com

## Subscriptions

InboxClarity offers a free Standard tier and optional Premium Monthly and Premium Yearly subscriptions (Standard is free for one account and rules-based triage. Premium Monthly and Premium Yearly unlock multiple accounts and on-device classification.). Payments are processed by **Google Play**, not by SnappyAI Solutions. Google Play's refund rules and consumer-rights law apply.

## Requesting a refund

1. First, open **Settings → Manage subscription** to review or cancel your plan. Cancellation stops future renewals; it does not automatically refund a past charge.
2. To request a refund for a current billing period, use Google Play's refund flow for the relevant order, or email snappyaisolutions@gmail.com with your Google Play order number and a short reason. We aim to acknowledge within 72 hours.
3. We may issue a refund at our discretion where the app did not perform as described, where a duplicate charge occurred, or where required by law. We cannot refund charges already processed by Google Play outside Google's own process.

## Price changes and grandfathering

If a price changes, we give at least **30 days' notice before your next renewal**, and your current term is never repriced mid-cycle. Active subscribers keep the features they had at purchase. If a price increase reaches you without the required notice, contact us and we will help you obtain a refund through Google Play.

## Free trials

Where a 21-day trial is offered, it converts to a paid subscription unless cancelled before the trial ends. Google Play shows the conversion date. If you were charged after cancelling in time, contact us with the order number.

## Chargebacks

Please contact us before raising a chargeback so we can try to resolve the issue. If a chargeback is raised, we will respond to Google Play with the relevant purchase and entitlement records. We do not penalise users who exercise a legitimate consumer right.

## Data after cancellation or refund

Cancelling or refunding never deletes your data. All local content, export, import, backup and delete features remain available in the Standard tier.
