# InboxClarity — Privacy Policy

**Effective:** 21 November 2026 · **Publisher:** SnappyAI Solutions · **Contact:** snappyaisolutions@gmail.com

## Who we are

SnappyAI Solutions ("we", "us") publishes InboxClarity ("the app"), on-device email triage with needs-reply classification and one-tap unsubscribe. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. If you have a question, write to snappyaisolutions@gmail.com.

## Scope

This policy applies to the app only. It does not cover Google Play or any third-party service you choose to connect (for example your own mail server or feed host), which are governed by their own policies.

## Data we process

Everything below is processed **on your device** unless stated otherwise.

| Data category | What it is | Where it is stored |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mail credentials | IMAP host, username and password you enter | On device, encrypted |
| Cached messages | Headers and bodies you choose to sync | On device, encrypted |
| Tasks, snoozes and rules | Items and classification rules you create | On device, encrypted |

We do not require an account, and we do not collect advertising identifiers, contact lists, location, or browsing history.

## Network access and third parties

This app connects only to the IMAP mail server you configure. Email content is never sent to SnappyAI Solutions or any third party.

The only third party involved in payments is Google Play Billing, which processes subscriptions under Google's own privacy policy. We do not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, crash-upload services or data brokers.


## On-device AI

Optional on-device classification and reply drafts run locally. Drafts are suggestions you edit; nothing is sent without your action.

Generated suggestions are always editable and are never treated as authoritative facts.

## Legal basis and your rights

We process your data to provide the app you asked for (performance of a contract) and, where applicable, because you have given consent for an optional feature such as calendar access or microphone use. Under data-protection law you may request access, correction, deletion, portability or restriction of processing.

You can exercise most of these rights directly in the app: use **Export** to take your data in an open format and **Delete all data** to erase it. For anything else, email snappyaisolutions@gmail.com. We aim to acknowledge requests within 72 hours.

## Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it. Some apps offer a retention setting (for example audio retention in MeetNotes or document count in LifeVault); where they do, the default is documented in the app. Uninstalling the app removes its local data; we do not hold a copy. Exported files are your copies and are stored wherever you put them.

## Children

The apps are not directed to children. HomeHub explicitly excludes child accounts.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version in the public documents repository and, for material changes, show a notice in the app before the change takes effect.
