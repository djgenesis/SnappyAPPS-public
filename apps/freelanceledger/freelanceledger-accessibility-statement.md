# FreelanceLedger — Accessibility Statement

**Effective:** 21 November 2026 · **Publisher:** SnappyAI Solutions · **Contact:** snappyaisolutions@gmail.com

## Commitment

SnappyAI Solutions wants FreelanceLedger to be usable by as many people as possible. We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 level AA and EN 301 549 where they apply to a mobile app, and we follow Android accessibility guidance.

## Measures taken

- **Screen readers:** controls are labelled for TalkBack; meaningful content has content descriptions and reading order.
- **Text and layout:** fonts scale to 200 % without clipping or loss of function; layouts adapt across compact, medium, expanded, large and extra-large window sizes.
- **Touch and input:** interactive targets are at least 48 × 48 dp with at least 8 dp spacing; keyboard and switch access are supported where the platform allows.
- **Contrast and colour:** text contrast is at least 4.5:1 and non-text contrast at least 3:1; dynamic colour is supported with accessible fallback palettes.
- **Motion:** spring-based motion respects the system reduced-motion setting; non-animated fallbacks are provided.
- **Language:** English and Greek are supported throughout, including store listing text and settings.

## Known limitations

Some advanced interactions may have simpler alternatives — for example drag-and-drop actions also have button-based controls. If you find a barrier, tell us and we will prioritise it.

## Feedback

Email snappyaisolutions@gmail.com with the app name and a description of the barrier. We aim to acknowledge within 72 hours. This statement is reviewed with each release.
