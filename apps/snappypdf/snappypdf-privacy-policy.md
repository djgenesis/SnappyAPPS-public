# Snappy PDF - Privacy Policy

**Effective:** 24 November 2026 | **Publisher:** SnappyAI Solutions | **Contact:** snappyaisolutions@gmail.com

> Draft generated from the portfolio template. Requires legal review before being linked from a Play listing.

Snappy PDF is a offline-first PDF reading, annotation, scanning, OCR and conversion .

## Data we process
All app content you create (documents, notes, settings and local models) is stored **on your device**. The app has no account and no advertising or analytics SDKs. If an optional online AI feature is offered, it runs only after an explicit per-action user decision and is disclosed before any content is sent. Payments are handled by Google Play under Google's own privacy policy.

## Your rights
You can export your data in an open format and delete it inside the app. For access, correction, portability or restriction requests, email snappyaisolutions@gmail.com. We aim to acknowledge requests within 72 hours.

## Retention and children
Data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. The apps are not directed at children.
