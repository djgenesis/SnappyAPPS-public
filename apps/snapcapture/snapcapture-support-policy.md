# SnapCapture — Support Policy

**Effective:** 21 November 2026 · **Publisher:** SnappyAI Solutions · **Contact:** snappyaisolutions@gmail.com

## How to get help

Email **snappyaisolutions@gmail.com** with the app name (SnapCapture) in the subject. You can also use the **Contact support** row in the app's Settings, which opens a prefilled email. Do not send sensitive information you are not comfortable sharing; for account-specific issues, include only what is needed.

## Response targets

We aim to acknowledge new requests within **72 hours** and provide a substantive response within **5 business days**. Complex issues may take longer; we will tell you if so. Support is provided on a best-effort basis in English and Greek.

## What we can help with

- Installation, permissions and first-run setup
- Billing, subscriptions, trials, cancellations and refunds (with Google Play)
- Sync or connectivity issues for the features that use a network
- Suspected bugs, crashes and data-export questions
- Reporting AI-generated output that needs review (where the app generates content)

## Diagnostics

The app contains **no telemetry**. If you report a problem, you may optionally use **Send diagnostics**, which shows you a preview of a local report and sends nothing until you confirm in your own email app. We will never ask you to disable your privacy settings.

## Supported versions

We support the current release and the previous minor version. Older versions may still work but may not receive fixes.

## Out of scope

We cannot provide medical, financial, tax or legal advice, guarantee third-party services you connect, or recover data you deleted without a backup.
