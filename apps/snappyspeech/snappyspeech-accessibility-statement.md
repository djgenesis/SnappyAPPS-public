# Snappy Speech - Accessibility Statement

**Effective:** 24 November 2026 | **Publisher:** SnappyAI Solutions | **Contact:** snappyaisolutions@gmail.com

> Draft generated from the portfolio template. Requires legal review before being linked from a Play listing.

Snappy Speech is a offline text-to-speech and speech-to-text with optional on-device models .

## Commitment
We aim to follow Material 3 Expressive accessibility guidance: TalkBack labels, 48 dp touch targets, support for 200% font scaling, sufficient contrast and reduced motion.

## Feedback
If you encounter an accessibility barrier, email snappyaisolutions@gmail.com or use the in-app feedback link. We aim to acknowledge accessibility reports within 72 hours.
